# Distribution Decision

Status: **NOT APPROVED**

This record governs whether Kinaraidee launches Commercial as Web/PWA only or also through native app stores. It must not be used to infer store-account readiness, policy approval, native build readiness, subscription approval, Public Beta completion, or Commercial GO.

## Decision fields
- Distribution mode (Web/PWA only, native stores, or phased): **UNSET**
- Distribution owner/approver: **UNSET**
- Google Play developer account status/reference: **UNSET**
- Apple Developer account status/reference: **UNSET**
- Native packaging/build approach: **UNSET**
- Store subscription/payment policy review reference: **UNSET**
- Store privacy/data-safety metadata review reference: **UNSET**
- Store support/contact metadata reference: **UNSET**
- Rollout/rollback approach by channel: **UNSET**
- Approved at: **UNSET**

## Approval requirements
Changing this record to **APPROVED** requires all decision fields above to be resolved with real, reviewable values. Approval only records a distribution decision; it does not prove store enrollment, app review approval, native build/test success, payment approval, or production rollout success.

## Evidence boundary
Current Web/PWA deployment evidence may support a Web/PWA distribution choice, but it does not establish Apple/Google account ownership, store policy compliance, store listing approval, native-device acceptance, in-app purchase approval, or Commercial GO.
