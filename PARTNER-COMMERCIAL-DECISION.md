## Approval rule

Do not change Status to **APPROVED** until every applicable field above contains a real reviewed value and the selected commercial model, evidence format, responsibilities, legal/privacy dependencies and reconciliation rules are traceable. If a field is genuinely not applicable, record the reviewed rationale explicitly rather than inventing a value.

Approval of this decision record does not itself prove that any restaurant agreed, any destination URL/menu mapping is correct, click/conversion tracking works under real traffic, pending/confirmed/cancelled lifecycle is correct, reconciliation occurred, an invoice/payment happened, revenue exists, Public Beta is complete, or Commercial GO is authorized. Those require separate partner-backed and execution evidence.

## Evidence boundary

Partner API hardening, rejection-only probes, Partner application form submissions, test data, CI, issue comments, draft commission hypotheses or this decision record are governance/technical evidence only. They do not prove a commercial agreement, partner readiness, conversion, commission liability, reconciliation, payment, revenue, Privacy/Legal approval, Public Beta completion, or Commercial GO.
