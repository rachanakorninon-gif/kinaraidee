# Kinaraidee — Marketing Creative Production Matrix

Status: **PRE-LAUNCH / PRODUCTION PREP ONLY**

Verified against official platform guidance on: **2026-08-29**

This document prepares reusable ad masters without launching spend, claiming unavailable store distribution, or implying Premium/prize entry is live.

## Current creative truth boundary

### Allowed now
- Product name: `กินอะไรดี`
- Hook: `วันนี้กินอะไรดี?`
- Product promise: `ไม่ต้องคิด ให้เราช่วยเลือก`
- Mechanism: `🎲 ไม่รู้เลย — เลือกให้ฉัน`
- Current CTA: `ลองใช้เลย` / `เปิดกินอะไรดี`
- Show actual product UI and actual menu-selection behavior.

### Not allowed now
- `ดาวน์โหลดเลย` when it implies App Store / Google Play distribution
- App Store badge
- Google Play badge
- claim that Premium payment is active
- claim that prize entries are open
- claim that an account signup creates prize eligibility
- invented download/member/Premium/eligible-user counts

**NO APP STORE BADGES. NO GOOGLE PLAY BADGES. NO LIVE PRIZE CLAIMS.**

## Master asset standard

Create one vertical master first:
- canvas: **1080 × 1920**
- aspect ratio: **9:16**
- master duration: **15 seconds**
- cutdown: **6 seconds**
- keep core message, logo, CTA and app UI inside platform safe zones
- preview in each platform's ad manager before trafficking
- subtitle/overlay copy must remain understandable with sound off
- audio/voice should add value when sound is on

## Current platform guidance snapshot

### TikTok
Official TikTok creative guidance supports full-screen vertical **9:16**. Current TikTok materials recommend native/social-first creative, safe-zone awareness and concise messaging. TikTok documentation varies by ad product on recommended duration, so Kinaraidee uses a 15-second internal master plus 6-second cutdown and re-checks the selected ad product immediately before launch.

Official references:
- https://ads.tiktok.com/business/library/7TopCreativeTips.pdf
- https://ads.tiktok.com/resources/help/article/global-app-bundle-video-ad-specifications

### Meta Reels
Meta recommends creative built for Reels, including **9:16 vertical video**, audio and keeping key messages in the safe zone. Reels default to a sound-on experience when the user has not muted sound.

Official reference:
- https://www.facebook.com/business/ads/facebook-instagram-reels-ads

### YouTube Shorts
Google recommends vertical **9:16** assets for Shorts. Current Google Ads guidance lists **1080 × 1920** as the recommended HD size for 9:16 vertical assets. Applicable video guidance supports short vertical assets for Shorts; Kinaraidee uses a 15-second master and 6-second cutdown.

Official references:
- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17091270
- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/16041697
- https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/17141078

Platform specifications change. Re-check official guidance immediately before trafficking.

## Creative set A — Direct pain

ID: `C001`

### 15-second storyboard
- 0–2s: `วันนี้กินอะไรดี?`
- 2–5s: quick social friction: `อะไรก็ได้...` / thinking face / scrolling food choices
- 5–8s: open Kinaraidee and show `🎲 ไม่รู้เลย — เลือกให้ฉัน`
- 8–12s: tap → show one real menu-result state
- 12–15s: brand + `ไม่ต้องคิด ให้เราช่วยเลือก` + `ลองใช้เลย`

Primary destination:
`https://rachanakorninon-gif.github.io/kinaraidee/`

### 6-second cutdown
- 0–2s: `กินอะไรดี?`
- 2–4s: tap `ไม่รู้เลย`
- 4–6s: result + `ลองใช้เลย`

## Creative set B — Social friction

ID: `C002`

### Hook
`กินอะไร?` → `อะไรก็ได้...`

### 15-second flow
- couple/friends/family cannot decide
- show phone opening Kinaraidee
- tap `ไม่รู้เลย`
- result appears
- CTA `ลองใช้เลย`

Do not imply the app resolves interpersonal conflict beyond helping choose a meal.

## Creative set C — Product mechanism

ID: `C003`

Screen-led creative with minimal copy:
- `คิดไม่ออก?`
- show app screen
- focus on `🎲 ไม่รู้เลย — เลือกให้ฉัน`
- result
- `กินอะไรดี — ลองใช้เลย`

This should be the cleanest control creative because it demonstrates the differentiating mechanism directly.

## Copy hierarchy

Keep on-screen Thai minimal to reduce rendering/readability errors:
1. hook
2. core mechanism
3. brand
4. CTA

Preferred phrases:
- `วันนี้กินอะไรดี?`
- `คิดไม่ออก?`
- `ไม่รู้เลย — เลือกให้ฉัน`
- `ไม่ต้องคิด ให้เราช่วยเลือก`
- `ลองใช้เลย`

## Creative QA checklist

Before any asset is marked traffic-ready:
- [ ] 9:16 master exists at 1080×1920
- [ ] 6-second cutdown exists if required by the media plan
- [ ] actual app UI/mechanism shown truthfully
- [ ] no App Store badge
- [ ] no Google Play badge
- [ ] no `ดาวน์โหลดเลย` claim implying unavailable store distribution
- [ ] no Premium-live claim
- [ ] no prize-entry-open claim
- [ ] no fabricated social proof/counts
- [ ] CTA points to the public root app, not a specific group invitation room
- [ ] important text/UI is inside platform safe zones
- [ ] asset previewed in the relevant ad manager before launch
- [ ] music/audio rights are cleared for commercial use
- [ ] final destination loads correctly on mobile
